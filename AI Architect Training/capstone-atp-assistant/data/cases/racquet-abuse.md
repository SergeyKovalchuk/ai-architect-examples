A player smashes a racquet or shouts an obscenity and receives a code violation. Code
violations follow a cumulative schedule: first offense is a warning, second is a point
penalty, third and beyond are game penalties, and egregious conduct can lead to default.
Categories include racquet/ball abuse, audible or visible obscenity, and verbal/physical
abuse. The chair umpire issues the violation; a supervisor is summoned for point penalties
and any decision that could lead to default.
