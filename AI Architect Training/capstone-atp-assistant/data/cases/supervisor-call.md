---
id: supervisor-call
title: When and how a supervisor or referee is called
tags: [supervisor, referee, appeal, procedure, rules dispute]
---
A player wants to call a supervisor. A player may request the chair umpire to summon the
supervisor when they believe a rule or procedure was applied incorrectly. The supervisor
rules on questions of rule interpretation and procedure but cannot overturn a point or a
judgment call about what the umpire saw. The supervisor may also be required for point
penalties, defaults, and unsafe-condition decisions. The referee is the final on-site
authority above the supervisor.
