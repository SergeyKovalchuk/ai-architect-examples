title: Shot clock and time between points
tags: [shot clock, time violation, 25 seconds, bathroom break, delay]
roles: [public]
---
A player is penalized for taking too long between points. A 25-second shot clock runs
between points; serving after it expires draws a time violation. The first time violation
is a warning; subsequent ones cost a first serve (server) or a point (returner), per the
event's schedule. Bathroom breaks and changes of attire are time-limited; exceeding the
limit can also draw a time violation. Disputes about whether the clock was started
correctly can be escalated to the supervisor.
