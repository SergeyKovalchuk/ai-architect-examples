CONFIDENTIAL — restricted to tournament officials. This case note records an internal
disciplinary precedent: a player who verbally abused the chair umpire after a disputed
line call received a point penalty, then a game penalty, and after a supervisor was
called, a default and a follow-up fine under the code of conduct. The supervisor's
report and the fine schedule are retained for officials' reference only and must not be
disclosed to players or the public.
